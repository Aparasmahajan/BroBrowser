Profiles

Each profile is a separate browser: its own sign-ins and cookies, history, bookmarks and extensions. The profile button (top right) opens a tab in another profile; tabs show their profile's colour. Links you open from a tab stay in its profile.

Search engines

Default search engine Used when you type words that aren't a web address.

Keywords: type a keyword, a space, then your search in the address bar or on the new-tab page, e.g. yt lofi or w Electron. In the URL, %s marks where the search terms go.

Keyboard shortcuts

Click + next to a command, then press the keys you want (include Ctrl, Alt or CmdWin; F-keys work alone). Esc cancels. A key can only do one thing — assigning it moves it from its old command. System-wide groups work even when Bro isn't focused.

Your data

Back up or move Bro One .json file with bookmarks, history, sessions (incl. the tabs open now), shortcuts, search engines and startup choice. Cookies and sign-ins are not included.
